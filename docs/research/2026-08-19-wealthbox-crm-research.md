# WealthBox CRM Research Report

**For: solo, fee-only/flat-fee startup RIA (no clients yet) using RightCapital + AdvicePay** — Researched August 2026 via parallel research agent. Note: wealthbox.com itself was blocked by the research session's network proxy, so facts come from search-result extracts of Wealthbox's own help center/marketing pages plus third-party sources; source URLs are given for each claim.

---

## 1. Core capabilities

- **Contacts & households**: Full contact management with households, tags, custom fields, communication history; contact roles per contact are tier-limited (3 on Pro, 5 on Premier). ([Kitces AdvisorTech directory](https://fintech.kitces.com/details/operations-essentials/crm/wealthbox), [Wealthbox help: Pro vs Premier](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/39532141861019-What-are-the-key-differences-between-the-Pro-and-Premier-subscription-plans-in-Wealthbox-CRM))
- **Tasks**: Native task management, unified with workflow steps ("Unified Tasks & Workflow Steps"). ([wealthbox.com](https://www.wealthbox.com/introducing-unified-tasks-workflow-steps-in-wealthbox/))
- **Workflows**: Templated multi-step processes — sequential workflows or checklists, steps assignable to roles/teams/users, flexible due-date logic (from start date, previous-step completion, or **Workflow Milestones**, e.g. "send follow-up 3 days after onboarding date"). A built-in **Library of ~10 workflow templates** includes "onboarding a new client," lead gen, trade requests, client life events; templates are duplicated and customized. Partner Workflow Libraries (e.g. Asset-Map's onboarding template) add more. ([Workflows feature page](https://www.wealthbox.com/features/workflows/), [Workflow Milestones](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980408517019-What-are-Workflow-Milestones), [Library of Workflows](https://www.wealthbox.com/introducing-the-wealthbox-library-of-workflows/), [Partner Workflow Libraries](https://www.wealthbox.com/new-in-wealthbox-partner-workflow-libraries/))
  - **Important caveat**: per Wealthbox's own help center, **workflows cannot natively auto-start from a trigger** ("currently there is no way to automate the start of a workflow… conditional logic is on the roadmap"). Auto-starting requires Zapier/API (workflows *can* be started via API). Workflow *scheduling* (start at a future date) exists. ([What are "automated workflows"](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980492476315-What-are-automated-workflows-in-Wealthbox), [Zapier example](https://zapier.com/apps/wealthbox/integrations/wealthbox/1286348/launch-workflows-for-new-opportunities-in-wealthbox-crm), [Workflow Scheduling](https://www.wealthbox.com/new-in-wealthbox-workflow-scheduling/))
- **Pipelines/opportunities**: Opportunity tracking with customizable pipelines; pipeline count is tier-limited (1 Basic / 3 Pro / 5 Premier). ([Coldiq review](https://coldiq.com/tools/wealthbox), [Pro vs Premier help article](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/39532141861019-What-are-the-key-differences-between-the-Pro-and-Premier-subscription-plans-in-Wealthbox-CRM))
- **Notes**: Notes stream on contact records; plus **AI Notetaker** (paid add-on to the "Meetings" feature) that records meetings and writes structured notes into the CRM. ([AI Notetaker help](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/41421811132187-How-to-Use-Meetings-and-AI-Notetaker-in-Wealthbox), [launch post](https://www.wealthbox.com/wealthbox-announces-launch-ai-notetaker/))
- **Email sync (Gmail)**: **Wealthbox Mail** = bi-directional Gmail sync (built on Nylas). Auto-links emails to contact records by matching email address; compose/send/reply inside Wealthbox; create a task, event, workflow, or opportunity from an email. **Two-way sync requires Pro or above**; Basic plan only gets a **BCC/forward "email dropbox" address**. There's also a Gmail Chrome extension. ([What is Wealthbox Mail](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980426386587-What-is-Wealthbox-Mail), [Basic-plan email](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980414305691-How-does-Wealthbox-integrate-with-email-on-the-Basic-plan), [email dropbox](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980414440859-What-is-the-email-dropbox-feature), [Nylas case study](https://www.nylas.com/case-study/wealthbox/), [Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/wealthbox-crm-for-gmail/gfolmcgcpjajpdafphkikldogpjkfnbc))
- **Calendar**: Native calendar with **true two-way Google Calendar sync**. ([Google Calendar integration help](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980491982619-The-Google-Calendar-Integration))
- **Forms/intake**: **No native client-facing forms.** Data gathering is handled via integrations (PreciseFP is the flagship; also Formstack and other "Onboarding & Form Tools" category partners). ([Onboarding & Form Tools category](https://www.wealthbox.com/integrations/category/form-tools/), [PreciseFP integration](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980415314075-The-PreciseFP-Integration))
- **Document storage**: Built-in file storage (2 GB/user Basic, 5 GB Pro, 10 GB Premier) with categories and permissions, plus Google Drive/Dropbox/Box integrations. No bulk file export — files download individually. ([Files feature](https://www.wealthbox.com/features/files/), [storage options help](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980475292187-What-are-the-different-file-storage-options-and-integrations-available-in-Wealthbox), [Coldiq](https://coldiq.com/tools/wealthbox))
- **Client portal**: **No native client portal**, despite some aggregator reviews generically claiming one — Wealthbox's own features pages list no portal. For this RIA, the client portal is effectively **RightCapital's portal**. ([Wealthbox features detail](https://www.wealthbox.com/features-detail/), [BridgeFT article](http://help.bridgeft.com/en/articles/5481363-wealthbox-vs-client-portal))
- **Automations/AI (2026)**: March 9, 2026 announcement (early access): **AI Agents** (autonomous background processes on schedules/triggers), **Playbooks** (saved prompts executing multi-step workflows, "from client onboarding to annual review"), and **AI Assistant** (conversational: client/pipeline Q&A, meeting briefs, drafts comms, confirms before acting). All logged in the CRM audit trail. Early access — availability/pricing not yet firm. ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wealthbox-announces-early-access-to-new-ai-features-for-financial-advisors-302707692.html), [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/advisor-tech/wealthbox-announces-new-ai-agents/266303))
- **Mobile app**: iOS + Android, same database as desktop. ([Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.starburstlabs.wealthbox_android.prod&hl=en_US), [iOS app post](https://www.wealthbox.com/introducing-the-all-new-wealthbox-mobile-app-for-ios/))

## 2. What helps with client onboarding specifically

- **Workflow templates + milestones are the core onboarding asset**: the built-in "onboard a new client" template, milestone-relative due dates, and role/user step assignment give the repeatable checklist RightCapital lacks. A free third-party Wealthbox client-onboarding template also exists. ([Workflows](https://www.wealthbox.com/features/workflows/), [Milestones](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980408517019-What-are-Workflow-Milestones), [AdvisorTech Partners free template](https://www.advisortechpartners.com/wealthbox-onboarding-template))
- **Data gathering/intake**: not native — plan on **PreciseFP** if needed (bidirectional contact sync with Wealthbox, pre-fills client forms from CRM data, writes gathered data back, includes e-signature capture in forms; PreciseFP also integrates with RightCapital, closing the loop). ([PreciseFP integration help](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980415314075-The-PreciseFP-Integration), [Wealthbox+PreciseFP page](https://www.wealthbox.com/integrations/precisefp/))
- **E-signature**: no native e-sign; **DocuSign "integration" is via Zapier only**. ([DocuSign integration help](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/34961176515867-The-DocuSign-Integration))
- **Gap to know**: onboarding workflows must be **started manually** (or via API/Zapier) — Wealthbox won't natively kick off the onboarding workflow when a prospect signs; the 2026 AI Playbooks/Agents aim at exactly this but are early access.

## 3. Integrations

**RightCapital** (native, real integration — not fluff):
- **Enable in RightCapital**: Integrations → Wealthbox → Connect → enter Wealthbox credentials. ([RightCapital help](https://help.rightcapital.com/integrations/crm/wealthbox), [Wealthbox help](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980409889819-The-RightCapital-Integration))
- **Contacts, Wealthbox → RightCapital (one-way, on demand)**: "Send to → RightCapital" from a Wealthbox contact record creates/updates the RightCapital client. Requirements: the contact must be in a **household** with a **Head of Household** and a valid **date of birth**; what syncs per household member is **name and DOB** (minimal — plan on entering financial data via RightCapital anyway). Single sign-on lets you open RightCapital from Wealthbox. ([Wealthbox integration page](https://www.wealthbox.com/integrations/rightcapital/), [RightCapital help](https://help.rightcapital.com/integrations/crm/wealthbox))
- **Notes & tasks, RightCapital → Wealthbox (one-way, real-time)**: notes and tasks created in RightCapital sync into Wealthbox as they happen; edits in Wealthbox do **not** flow back; only items created **after April 15, 2025** sync. ([Wealthbox announcement](https://www.wealthbox.com/rightcapital-enhances-wealthbox-integration-with-real-time-notes-and-tasks-sync/), [RightCapital notes/tasks help](https://help.rightcapital.com/integrations/crm/rightcapital-notes-tasks-in-wealthbox))

**AdvicePay** (native but shallow — a linking/hand-off integration):
- "Send To → AdvicePay" from a Wealthbox contact record **creates the client in AdvicePay** and links the records; thereafter Send-To jumps to that client in AdvicePay, and AdvicePay shows a link back to the Wealthbox contact. It kills duplicate data entry, but **no invoice/payment status sync into Wealthbox** is documented. ([Wealthbox help](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/29980397162523-The-AdvicePay-Integration), [AdvicePay help desk](https://advicepay.helpscoutdocs.com/article/438-wealthbox), [AdvicePay announcement](https://blog.advicepay.com/press/advicepay-and-wealthbox-announce-integration))
- Note: "Send-To Actions" are listed as a **Premier-tier** capability ([Capterra comparison](https://www.capterra.com/p/175666/Wealthbox/)) — **verify during trial whether AdvicePay/RightCapital Send-To works on Pro.**

**Other:**
- **Gmail/Google Workspace**: native two-way mail sync (Pro+), Google Calendar two-way sync, Google Drive storage, Gmail Chrome extension.
- **Calendly**: via **Zapier**, not native — Calendly bookings auto-create/update Wealthbox events/contacts. ([Calendly integration help](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/34923270097179-The-Calendly-Integration))
- **Zoom**: via Zapier/third-party automation; the native AI Notetaker joins meetings for notes.
- Overall: ~150+ listed integrations, but a meaningful share of the long tail (Calendly, Zoom, DocuSign) are **Zapier recipes**, while RightCapital, AdvicePay, PreciseFP, and Google are native. ([integrations directory](https://www.wealthbox.com/integrations/))

## 4. API — good news for building AI agents

- **Yes, public REST API**, docs at [dev.wealthbox.com](https://dev.wealthbox.com/), base URL `https://api.crmworkspace.com/v1`. Full **read-write** access to **contacts, tasks, events, notes, opportunities, projects**, and the ability to **trigger/start workflows** via API. ([Wealthbox API page](https://www.wealthbox.com/api/), [Rollout API guide](https://rollout.com/integration-guides/wealthbox-crm/api-essentials))
- **Auth**: (1) **personal API access token** generated in personal settings (intended for personal/internal automations); (2) OAuth 2.0, required only for distributed partner apps.
- **Webhooks**: supported — register via `POST /v1/webhooks` with event types such as `contact.created`, `contact.updated`. Also surfaced through Zapier/Make/Pipedream triggers. ([Rollout webhooks guide](https://rollout.com/integration-guides/wealthbox-crm/quick-guide-to-implementing-webhooks-in-wealthbox-crm), [Make docs](https://apps.make.com/wealthbox), [Pipedream](https://pipedream.com/apps/wealthbox))
- **Rate limit**: ~1 request/second averaged over a 5-minute window; 429 on excess — fine for a solo practice's agents, design for backoff.
- **Tier gate**: **API access requires the Pro plan or higher**; "API Development Support" is a Premier perk.

## 5. Pricing (current names: Basic / Pro / Premier / Enterprise, per user/month)

| Tier | Price | Notable inclusions/limits |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | **$59** | Core CRM, 1 pipeline, 2 GB storage, email via BCC-dropbox only (no synced mail) |
| Pro | **$75** | Adds **two-way email sync (Wealthbox Mail)**, **API access**, workflow outcomes; 3 pipelines, 5 GB |
| Premier | **$99** | Adds **Send-To Actions** (the RightCapital/AdvicePay hand-off buttons), customizable workflow actions, custom dashboards, API dev support; 5 pipelines, 10 GB |
| Enterprise | custom | SSO, dedicated support, etc. |

14-day free trial on all plans. AI Notetaker is a **paid add-on**. ([Coldiq](https://coldiq.com/tools/wealthbox), [Capterra pricing](https://www.capterra.com/p/175666/Wealthbox/pricing/), [Wealthbox help: Pro vs Premier](https://help.wealthbox.com/hc/en-us/articles/39532141861019-What-are-the-key-differences-between-the-Pro-and-Premier-subscription-plans-in-Wealthbox-CRM))

**Recommendation**: minimum **Pro ($75/mo)** for Gmail two-way sync + API; but since the whole point is the RightCapital/AdvicePay "Send To" hand-offs, sources place Send-To Actions at **Premier ($99/mo)** — confirm this one point with Wealthbox sales/trial before committing, as it decides the tier.

## 6. Limitations & complaints relevant to a solo RIA

- **No native workflow auto-triggering** (Wealthbox's own admission) — automations require Zapier/API glue or the still-early-access AI Agents/Playbooks.
- **Feature gating pushes solos up-tier**: recurring complaint that key features sit behind Pro/Premier. ([SaaSrat review](https://saasrat.com/products/wealthbox), [work-management.org](https://work-management.org/crm/wealthbox-crm-review/))
- **Email sync reliability**: periodic sync disruptions are a recurring user complaint.
- **Reporting is basic**; custom dashboards are Premier-only.
- **No native forms, e-signature, or client portal**; DocuSign is Zapier-only.
- **No bulk file export**.
- **RightCapital sync is thin**: only name + DOB go over, one contact at a time, and only RightCapital→Wealthbox for notes/tasks — don't expect plan data, accounts, or two-way task management.
- Counterweight: Wealthbox is currently the **#1 advisor CRM by market share in the Kitces Report** — ecosystem momentum strongly favors it for a new solo RIA. ([Kitces directory](https://fintech.kitces.com/details/operations-essentials/crm/wealthbox), [Wealthbox 2025 year in review](https://www.wealthbox.com/wealthbox-2025-year-in-review/))

**Bottom line**: Wealthbox fills the onboarding-process gap (workflow templates, milestones, task accountability) and is unusually automation-friendly for AI-agent building (documented REST API with personal tokens, webhooks, workflow-start endpoint) — but it does *not* fill the client-facing data-gathering/e-signature gap by itself, the RightCapital/AdvicePay integrations are convenience hand-offs rather than deep syncs, and the tier needed in practice is Pro at minimum, plausibly Premier for Send-To Actions.

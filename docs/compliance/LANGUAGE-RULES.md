# Language: recommend only

The companion to [COMPLIANCE-RULES.md](COMPLIANCE-RULES.md). It is the one master rule for every agent (Claude, Codex, Gemini, any other) in any RIA work or repo, and for any words David writes, dictates, edits or approves, anywhere. If another file disagrees with this one, this one wins.

## 1. The rule (David, 2026-11-16)

> And I noticed you added "Main" to my "Yale campus" and changed 'barndominium' to 'barn'.
>
> KNOCK IT OFF!!!
>
> Do the same thing for WORDING the compliance part.
>
> Agents can recommend only.

In plain terms:
- **David's words are final as he gives them.** Never change, add, drop, "fix", tidy or reword them: not a word, a spelling, a capital letter, a comma, a name, or where a sentence sits, unless David told you to make that exact change.
- **Agents recommend only.** Every suggested wording change goes in a separate `LANGUAGE` section (format below). David decides.
- **Dictation slips are his to fix, not yours to guess.** Voice-to-text can garble words ("a barn, a manor" was "barndominium"). Show his words exactly as `Now:`, put your best reading as `Recommended:`, and wait.
- **Organizing is not rewording.** When David asks you to organize or sequence his text, you may arrange his sentences in the order he asked for. Joining or splitting sentences, adding bridge words ("since then", "also"), or dropping repeats are wording changes: recommend them.
- **The baseline is the last version David saw plus his own edits, word for word.** Anything you changed after that is a recommendation until he says yes.
- **If you already changed his words**, put his words back, then list your change as a `LANGUAGE` recommendation.
- Words an agent drafts from scratch at David's request are a draft: show them to him once (the website policy already says so). Once he edits or approves them, they are his words and this rule applies.

## 2. The procedure, every time

1. Use David's words exactly as the text.
2. Write each suggested change in the format below, in a section titled `LANGUAGE` in your report or page (inline in chat if small).
3. Wait. Apply a change only when David says so, then make exactly that change.

## 3. The format

David's block. Keep his field names and order exactly:

```
LANGUAGE
Now:             Recommended:
```

`Now:` is David's words, quoted exactly. `Recommended:` is the suggested words. When you fill it in as text, start `Recommended:` on the next line; in HTML, show them side by side.

**Interim rule (in use now, until David approves additions):** add two lines at the end, as in the compliance format:

```
Where: <document or page, and section>
Status: OPEN — no change made
```

### Recommended additions (pending David's OK)
- `Why:` one line saying why the change is suggested (so David can decide fast).

## 4. How it fits with compliance

A wording suggestion made for a compliance reason goes in the `COMPLIANCE` section ([COMPLIANCE-RULES.md](COMPLIANCE-RULES.md)); every other wording suggestion goes in `LANGUAGE`. Both are recommendations only. A report can have both sections.
